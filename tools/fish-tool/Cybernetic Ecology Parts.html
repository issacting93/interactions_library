Adding a piece

  1. Draw the left side only. The right is mirrored. Heads, plates and studs are centre pieces: draw them symmetric.
  2. Head points up, tail down, at the scale of the creature it belongs to.
  3. Colour by slot so it imports with the right motion: head body wing hind fin whisker tail; no fill for eye rings.
  4. Drop an orange dot (#FFB86B) where a fin or whisker joins the body.
  5. Send the SVG. Rig the motion afterwards in Wing Rig if you like.
