Grid

Each glyph is a walk along the grid with one optional branch. It is kept only if it spans the full height, crosses itself no more than allowed, and differs from every glyph already kept by at least Distinct strokes.

Stroke

Creature ends each open stroke in a hook and seats a bead on each junction. Beaded strings small parts along the strokes. Ornament adds barbs off the strokes, ringed corners, a mark above or below each letter, and at higher settings an engraved double line. Tap a glyph on the sheet to redraw just that letter.
