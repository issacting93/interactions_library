Knob

A 270° rotary readout. The value arc starts from the parameter’s natural zero: 0 dB, centre pan, or the bottom of the sweep. Value changes tween with anime.js (outExpo, 460 ms). Give it onInput and it becomes a slider: drag vertically (hold Shift for fine control) or use the arrow keys.

LIVE · DRAG OR ARROW KEYS
EVERY UNIT · MINI (NODE CARD) AND FULL (INSPECTOR)
