Inspector

Details for the current selection, plus the processing order and the patching rules. A selected node shows its role, live parameter controls, a port table and its latency. A selected cable shows its kind, its route and what that kind means. The panel rebuilds with a staggered entrance only when its shape changes; parameter edits sync in place.
