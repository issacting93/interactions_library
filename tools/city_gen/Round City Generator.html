Each sprite faces the same way: the inner edge (toward the core) is at the bottom, the outer edge at the top, counter-clockwise on the left and clockwise on the right. Pieces marked “+ mirror” also exist flipped.

Generator parameters

Grammar

Six kinds of rule decide what can go where. The street plan is solved first on the ground level; a second pass stacks storeys, roofs and towers on top of it.

1 · Sockets must match

Two neighbouring pieces must show the same label on the edge they share.

LabelMeaningPassable

2 · Ring zoning

Each ring only accepts pieces from its own district, so the city reads inside-out like the Round City of Baghdad.

RingCellsDistrictPieces

3 · Splitting outward

Where a ring has twice the cells of the ring inside it, a piece's outer edge touches two pieces, and both must match it. This keeps cells a similar size from centre to wall.

RINGS = [1, 8, 16, 32, 32, 32, 32, 64, 64]

4 · Fixed positions

ring 8, j ∈ {0, 16, 32, 48}  →  gate only
ring 8, any other j          →  never a gate
cells on a gate's bearing    →  street in and out
                                (the four royal avenues)

5 · Everything reaches the core

After solving, a walk from the Great Mosque follows plaza, street and arcade edges. Streets it never reaches are re-solved as closed buildings, keeping every socket the rest of the city already fixed.

—

6 · Stacking

Every cell is a column of four slots above its ground piece. A module's bottom socket must equal the top socket of what is below it, so a tower has to end in a cap and a storey has to end in a roof, dome, windcatcher or talar.

F  floor that can carry more    A  open sky
T  tower shaft                  W  wall walk
G  gate top

side:  a open · f built · k sabat bridge
       k meets only f or k  (a sabat needs a
       built floor on every side at its level)
DistrictMax levels above ground

Storeys get rarer the higher they go and the farther the cell is from the core, so the skyline climbs toward the mosque.

Solver

Lowest weighted entropy first, weighted random pick, arc-consistency propagation, and full backtracking from a decision stack when a cell runs out of options.
