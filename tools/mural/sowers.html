The Sowers and the Hollow

A second mythos, engraved in black and white from every Forge. Makers who seed life by giving themselves to it, a hollow thing that follows the seed, and the mercy that ends both.
Grinding the pigments…

IThe Sowing

Before the stars cooled, the Sowers hung a seed-wheel over a black sea. The first of them knelt at the water's edge and unravelled into it, and from the threads of its body, life rose.

IIThe Hollowing

Their children built towers to the sky. But something followed the seed: a pale root that grew up out of the ground and through everything living, and left it hollow, wearing the shape of what it ate.

IIIThe Silence

The last Sowers lit the Wheel. Its light passed over every world and every living thing fell still, so the Hollow would starve. They called it mercy. The last Sower sat down in stone, holding the one seed that was kept.

Made from: Sigil Forge (the seed-wheel, the Wheel and its twelve lesser seals), Spire Forge (the tower city), Creature Arch and Creature Classical (the ruins), Cybernetic Ecology (the first creatures), Scriptorium (the friezes, and the chapter names: spirit · sea · before, beast · city · night, sun · fall · after). An original myth on the themes of precursor stories, after Alien's Engineers and Halo's Forerunners; none of their creatures or ships are drawn.
