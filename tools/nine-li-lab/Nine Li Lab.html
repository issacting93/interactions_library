Four passes

The Kaogong ji fixes the plan: a 49 × 49 grid, nine avenues each way, palace on the axis, temple left, altar right, court in front, market behind. Then four passes run on the 56 free wards.

1 · Plan collapse

Wave function collapse lays out lanes only: plot, plot on a lane, lane, bend, junction, crossroads. Lanes leave a ward only through a gate at the middle of a side, and lanes that never reach a gate are closed off.

2 · Space syntax

Each ward becomes an access graph. A gate lane has depth 1, each further lane cell adds 0.5 (a lane is one space), entering a plot from a lane adds 1, and passing through another courtyard adds 1 more. The depth picks what goes on each plot:

3 · Model synthesis

Paul Merrell's model synthesis extends wave function collapse to 3D modules. Every cell gets four levels above its ground storey, and each module has six faces. The city starts from a trivially valid stack (one roof, open sky) and is then re-solved in overlapping 8 × 8 blocks; a block that fails keeps its previous solution, so the whole never breaks.

vertical   F floor  A sky  T pagoda shaft
           Lns / Lew  a lane running N–S / E–W
side       a open  w wall  g gallery joint (meets w only)

storey, balcony, double eave   F → F
gable, hip-and-gable, pyramid  F → A
pagoda tier F→T→T, spire T → A
gallery over a lane: sits on Lns or Lew and
needs a storey wall on both sides

Shallow, commercial plots stack more storeys; galleries are twice as likely in the market.

4 · MAP-Elites

A genome is a seed plus six numbers: gate chance, lane bias, storey bias, gallery bias, monasteries and gardens. New genomes are mutated from random elites. Each city lands in one of 36 cells by its height and mean access depth, and replaces that cell's elite only if it scores higher.

fitness = 0.45 × lanes kept
        + 0.35 × programme variety (normalised entropy)
        + 0.20 × min(1, galleries / 12)

Lattice patterns

Chinese window lattice and paving, drawn by rule. Ice-ray follows George Stiny's 1977 shape grammar of Daniel Sheets Dye's lattice catalogue: keep splitting a polygon with a line between two of its edges.
