Naqsh Forge

Every pattern family in this repo's Islamic/Persian ornament tools — Girih tiling, Banna'i brickwork, Eslimi scrollwork, Tongbori cut vessels, the Facade compositor, plus Karbandi lattice and Gothic Rosette tracery — as wireable nodes. Connect generators into the Radial Scatter or Region Fill composites to build combinations none of the standalone tools could make alone.
