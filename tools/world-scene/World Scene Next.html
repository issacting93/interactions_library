Camera

Drag the tank to turn it; Shift-drag to pan. Follow rides behind one creature. Re-centre clears any pan. Tilt now runs all the way to 90° -- push it there to bring the camera down level with the tank for a flat, 2D-looking side view (pairs well with Orthographic below).

Lens

Same orbit, tilt and zoom either way -- Orthographic just drops the perspective fall-off, so distant and near parts of the tank read at the same scale (Top view already looks orthographic-ish from directly overhead; this is the difference everywhere else).
