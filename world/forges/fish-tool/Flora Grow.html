The recipe

    
A recipe is data, like a hand-composed plant's list of placements. FloraGrow.grow(recipe) turns it into the same record the fifteen kit plants have, so EcologyFlora.plant stands it with no other change. Each part stays a flat drawing, but in a plane of its own that can face any way: leaves lie level, petals cup, stems are drawn crossed. The far side of a plant is drawn dimmer than the near side.
