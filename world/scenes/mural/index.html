The Tower of the Old Ones

A creation myth painted from every Forge: the tower, the seals, the ruins, the creatures and the script are all generated.
Grinding the pigments…

IThe First Light

Before the sand there was a seal of fire. The Old Ones poured out of it like sparks and learned to swim the sky.

IIThe Tower

A people raised a tower whose crown could hold the light. Through its beam they spoke with the Old Ones, who circled it trailing cloth.

IIIThe Long Sleep

The city sank, its columns left standing like tombstones. The pilgrims still walk back across the dunes, toward the light.

Made from: Spire Forge (the towers), Sigil Forge (the sun-seal and frieze seals), Creature Arch and Creature Classical (the ruins), Cybernetic Ecology (the Old Ones), Scriptorium (the glyphs and the chapter names, which spell sun · spirit · before, city · sky · spirit, and night · fall · after).
