Night Route

Three studies after Kentucky Route Zero: flat planes stacked by depth, one pale moon, one warm light, small human marks in a lot of dark. Loading the parts…

IThe Road at Zero

A still tableau. A Forge tower on the ridge with one lit window, an Old One crossing the moon, a truck coming over the crest under a light nobody hung.

IIDesktop

A new picture every day, seeded by the date: the moon is that night's real phase, the palette turns over, and sometimes a tower or an Old One is out there. The clock is live.

IIIThe Stage

The scene changes the way Route Zero's do, in plain view: the lights dip, the flats fly up into the loft on their wires and the next set comes down. Three acts, then round again with new parts.

Made from: Spire Forge (the towers), Creature Arch and Creature Classical (the station halls), Cybernetic Ecology (the Old Ones), all cut to flat silhouettes. After Kentucky Route Zero by Cardboard Computer: its theatrical sets, hidden lights and fixed framing.
