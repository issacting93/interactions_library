Design Research in the Age of AI — Component Library

The reusable visual system behind Design Research in the Age of AI (2).pdf (39 content slides + appendix, 16:9, by Issac Ting, 2026) — pure black ground, one monospace face, letter-spaced micro-labels, hairline rules, a three-colour "part" system for the deck's three acts, and an eight-colour categorical palette carried across every diagram.

Load tokens.css then components.css, drop markup onto a .ds-stage (1280×720), and compose from the classes demonstrated below. This is the source-deck system, native 16:9 — for the 9:16 vertical port used in Instagram video exports, see ../templates/research-core.css instead.
