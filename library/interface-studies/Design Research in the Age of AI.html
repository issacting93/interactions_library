Design Research in the Age of AI30 / 48
PART 2 · TRUST

Trust needs both

Communicating trust

A clear identity, tone and voice
Honest signals of what it can and can’t do
The same behaviour, every time
The trap: promising more than it delivers

Being trustworthy

Show what it can do, and how well
Let people check, override and undo
Recover gracefully from errors
Measure wellbeing, not just engagement
Only communicate the trust you’ve earned. That’s calibrated trust.
