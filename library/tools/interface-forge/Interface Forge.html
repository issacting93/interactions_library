Interface Forge

The fish-tool's Species Forge recombines pre-drawn rig parts because every sigil shares one slot format. Nothing here does — interface-studies and Instagram_Visuals/pieces are each a bespoke sketch (ophanim, compass, gear-lock, rosette, daisy-wheel, signal-wheel, reliquary…) with its own code. So this forge gives them a shared vocabulary instead: five fixed radial bands, a center motif, and a frame bezel, drawn fresh per genome. It mutates and crosses these assemblies, keeps the most coherent one for every cell of the map, and lets you breed by eye.

How one generation works

1 · Select

Pick a parent

A random elite from the map. Three times in ten it is crossed with a second elite first: each ring slot's kind is taken whole from one parent or the other, so inner rings only ever trade with inner rings.

2 · Mutate

Pull one lever

Re-kind a ring, resize or re-duty it, nudge its radius or phase, drop or restore a slot, swap the center motif, change the radial count, or move the whole dial onto a different frame.

3 · Assemble

Fill fixed bands

Each ring lives at a fixed radius band, so slots never renegotiate position — only what fills them changes. The center motif and frame bezel are laid in last.

4 · Judge

Keep it if it reads as an instrument

Rules stand in for "reads as one instrument": nothing crowds the hub, no ring motif stamped everywhere, no bare or overcrowded dial, lines thick enough to register. It competes only inside its own cell.
